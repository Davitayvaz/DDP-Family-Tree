How to use this family tree

  • Add a person: double-click empty space on the paper, or click "+ Add person".
  • Move a person: drag their card.
  • Resize a card: drag the small corner handle at its bottom-right.
  • Connect parent → child: drag the green dot under a card onto another card.
  • Edit name or note: double-click the text.
  • Card options: hover a card and click the ⋮ button for photo, background color, outline, black & white (for someone who's passed), and see-through.
  • Select several people: shift-click cards, or shift-drag an empty area to draw a selection box. Drag any selected card to move the group together, or use "Delete selected".
  • Style a connection line: click it for Curvy / Straight / Zigzag / 90° options, or to delete it.
  • Label a connection: double-click the line (e.g. "married", "divorced").
  • Zoom: use the +/− buttons, or hold Ctrl (⌘ on Mac) and scroll.
  • Canvas background: pick a color, or upload a background image.
  • Save your work: "Save file" downloads a .json you can reload later with "Load file". The tree also autosaves in this browser.
  • Print: "Print" auto-crops to your tree, switches to landscape, and uses whatever zoom level you were last on.
